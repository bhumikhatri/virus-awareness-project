🦠 What is a Computer Virus?

A computer virus is a type of malicious software that can damage files, affect computer performance, or spread to other computers.

Viruses can enter a computer through unsafe downloads, infected files, email attachments, or unknown USB devices.

⚠️ Warning Signs of a Virus

🐌 Slow Computer

Your computer may become unusually slow.

📢 Strange Pop-ups

Unexpected advertisements or messages may appear.

📁 Missing Files

Files may become unavailable or behave unexpectedly.

💻 Crashes

Applications may stop working or the system may crash.

🦠 Common Types of Malware

Virus

Can attach itself to files and spread when they are opened.

Worm

Can spread automatically between computers or networks.

Trojan

Malicious software disguised as a legitimate program.

Ransomware

Can block access to files and demand payment.

🛡️ How to Stay Safe

✅ Keep your operating system and applications updated.

✅ Use trusted security software.

✅ Avoid unknown links and suspicious downloads.

✅ Don't open unexpected email attachments.

✅ Scan USB drives before opening files.

✅ Keep important files backed up.

✅ Use strong and unique passwords.

⭐ Quick Safety Tips

🔄 Update Software

Keep your operating system and applications updated.

🔐 Use Strong Passwords

Use strong, unique passwords for your important accounts.

📧 Check Emails

Be careful with unexpected links and attachments.

💾 Backup Data

Keep backup copies of important files.
